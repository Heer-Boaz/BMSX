## What FLAG does NOT establish

- ABC's nonzero FLAG does not remove A or reject its triangle: A is visibly present in SXY0 despite deliberate H=2*Z divide overflow. No depth, IR, screen saturation, or MAC overflow occurs in this finite set.
- FLAG0 is not proof of satisfying cartridge Znear256: the divider condition compares H with2*SZ3, not Z with that software plane.
- Znear256 is a cartridge geometry choice, not a GTE register, implicit clip threshold, or BIOS API. Software can replace ABC with ordered quad D,B,C,E and triangles DBC+DCE. The exact edge intersections follow t=(256-128)/(512-128)=1/3; D/E are supplied vertices, not hardware-generated outputs.
- This is mapped TS owner execution plus paired C++ source verification, NOT native-conformance or cartridge-render evidence. Root's independent actual-cart/image evidence must be cited separately.
